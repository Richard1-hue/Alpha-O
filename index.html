Privacy

Your chat history is saved in this browser. To answer, messages go through a Cloudflare Worker to Google Gemini, Groq, or Cloudflare Workers AI. For analytics, Alpha-O records a random browser ID, visit and request timestamps, response status, provider, and a short excerpt (up to 200 characters) of your latest prompt. The backend supports 90-day analytics cleanup; that cleanup must be enabled in Cloudflare before automatic deletion begins. Full conversations and attached files are not stored in the analytics database. Please do not share passwords or private information.
